# Statistical Test Results Analysis: Test vs Control

## Key Finding: Trade-offs Accompanying Completion Rate Improvement

**The Test group shows higher completion rates across all segments, BUT this improvement comes with measurable costs in both duration and backward navigation.**

### Overall Pattern Across All Segments

| Metric | Test vs Control | Magnitude |
|--------|----------------|-----------|
| **Completion Rate** | Higher in Test | +3.2 to +4.1 percentage points |
| **Process Duration** | Higher in Test | +24 to +47 seconds longer |
| **Backward Navigation Rate** | Higher in Test | +4.2 to +9.5 percentage points |

All differences are statistically significant (p < 0.00001).

---

## Detailed Findings by Metric

### 1. Completion Rate
Test group shows consistently higher completion rates:
- **High age**: 67.1% (Test) vs 63.8% (Control) — **+3.2 pts**
- **Low age**: 71.5% (Test) vs 67.4% (Control) — **+4.1 pts**
- **High tenure**: 68.5% (Test) vs 65.0% (Control) — **+3.5 pts**
- **Low tenure**: 70.1% (Test) vs 66.2% (Control) — **+3.9 pts**

### 2. Process Duration (seconds)
Test group takes significantly longer to complete:
- **High age**: 381 sec (Test) vs 334 sec (Control) — **+47 sec (+14%)**
- **Low age**: 276 sec (Test) vs 252 sec (Control) — **+24 sec (+10%)**
- **High tenure**: 337 sec (Test) vs 300 sec (Control) — **+38 sec (+13%)**
- **Low tenure**: 319 sec (Test) vs 288 sec (Control) — **+32 sec (+11%)**

### 3. Backward Navigation Rate
Test group navigates backward substantially more often:
- **High age**: 37.9% (Test) vs 27.4% (Control) — **+10.5 pts (+38%)**
- **Low age**: 28.9% (Test) vs 24.7% (Control) — **+4.2 pts (+17%)**
- **High tenure**: 34.7% (Test) vs 25.2% (Control) — **+9.5 pts (+38%)**
- **Low tenure**: 32.1% (Test) vs 27.0% (Control) — **+5.1 pts (+19%)**

---

## Segment Patterns

While all segments show the same directional pattern (Test improves completion but increases duration and backward navigation), the data shows variation in magnitude:

- **High age segment** shows the largest duration increase (+47 seconds) and largest backward navigation increase (+10.5 pts)
- **Low age segment** shows smaller increases in both duration (+24 seconds) and backward navigation (+4.2 pts)
- Similar patterns appear in tenure segments

**Note:** This data does not explain why these differences exist across segments — further investigation would be needed to understand the underlying causes.

---

## Summary

The Test variation achieves its goal of improving completion rates by 3-4 percentage points. However, this comes with consistent trade-offs: processes take 10-14% longer to complete, and users navigate backward 17-38% more frequently. These trade-offs are present across all segments analyzed, though their magnitude varies.
